01 — The problem

Fishing vessels generate valuable data. Most of it is lost at sea.

While compliance requirements grow, the data needed to meet them is already being produced on board. It simply never reaches the people who can act on it.
  1. 01 Compliance EU traceability mandate from January 2026 — data required, not yet captured at sector scale.
  2. 02 Operational cost Margins erode trip by trip with no per-haul fuel evidence on board.
  3. 03 Political risk Quota debates run without operational evidence from the fleet.
  4. 04 Scientific gap Stock models rely on infrequent surveys, not continuous fleet data.

NaviTools was built to capture this missing data layer.

02 — For the fisherman

The technology that takes work off the deck.

NaviTools was conceived by a former skipper. Every feature is measured against one question: does it save time, money, or trouble on board?
Direct value, every trip

Less paperwork. More margin. Real protection.

  • Your e-logbook data, prepared for you. Position, depth, gear, species, weights and discards are captured from sensors all trip long. When it is time to submit the e-logbook, the fields are ready — you confirm, you do not type.
  • Full EU traceability, hold to buyer. NaviTools delivers the complete traceability solution the EU now requires. Each box is fixed to a position in the hold (FishGrid), scanned once by RFID at landing, and tracked through the auction to the buyer — end-to-end, on every trip. Already implemented on five vessels (below).
  • Fuel cost per haul, per trip, per kilo of fish. Liters per hour live. Liters per kilo of catch the moment a haul comes on deck. Spot the tow that earned nothing. See in euros whether a hull cleaning, a new propeller or a different RPM actually paid back.
  • Evidence on file before it's asked for. Net damage, gear loss, auction-weight disputes, near-misses: position, depth, course, hydraulic pressure and per-box catch composition are timestamped and stored. Insurance, surveyor, lawyer — the record is there when they need it.
  • Your grounds, mapped from your own hauls. Catch-per-hour by depth, bottom type and season — built trip after trip from data you already generate. The pattern no skipper can hold in their head over a 20-year career, written down and searchable.
  • Margin per trip, while you are still fishing. Catch value minus fuel burn minus crew share, recalculated as each haul lands. Visserij Verduurzaamt premium tracked separately. Stay or steam — decide with numbers, not just gut feel.

06 — In active development & deployment

Agentic AI and advanced modelling

AI agents handle the heavy work: crunching trip data, building dashboards, running predictive models that sharpen as more vessels contribute. On deck, the same agents make that data accessible in plain language — a skipper or fleet manager can ask a question and get an answer on their own phone or tablet. No new interface to learn — the data comes to them.
